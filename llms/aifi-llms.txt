# AiFi — Spaces Made Intelligent

> AiFi is the world's largest Spatial Intelligence platform. It uses
> camera-only computer vision to understand human behavior in physical
> spaces — translating movement, interaction, identity, and attention
> into decisions, experiences, and operational outcomes across retail,
> venues, government, travel, and beyond.

## Mission

To provide a digital understanding of what is happening in the
physical world and make that accessible and useful.

## Vision

A world where the physical world is as searchable, observable, and
understandable as the digital one. A world where operators no longer
rely on guesswork, point-in-time audits, or fragmented signals to
understand what is happening in their environments. Instead, insight
into the physical world becomes instant and effortless.

In this world, information about human movement, activity, and
operations is available in the blink of an eye. Every moment that
once faded into invisibility is now visible, contextual, and
actionable. Environments adapt in real time. Decisions become more
confident. Service becomes more personal. Standards rise naturally
across every location.

Manual, repetitive, and robotic tasks done by people disappear.
Employees gain time and clarity. Guests enjoy smoother, more
personalized experiences. Operators unlock capabilities that were
previously impossible — from continuously optimized operations to
consistent delivery across their entire footprint.

This is a world where physical environments become intelligent. Where
the tools we use in the digital world — instant queries,
personalization, and real-time understanding — finally extend into
the places where we work, shop, travel, learn, and connect.

When understanding the physical world becomes universally intuitive
and valuable, every experience becomes better for operators,
employees, and the people they serve.

This is the world AiFi is creating.

## What AiFi Actually Is

AiFi is not a collection of point solutions. It is a behavioral
intelligence platform with four layers:

- **Perception**: Video ingestion and feature extraction from camera
  networks. No shelf sensors required. No biometrics by default.
- **Behavior**: Nine domains of human behavior understanding —
  Movement & Flow, Presence & Dwell, Interaction, Sequence &
  Progression, Attention & Perception, Coordination & Load, Friction
  & Breakdown, Identity & Continuity, and Variability & Change.
- **Reasoning**: A real-time engine that answers: what is happening
  now (State), what should happen (Expectation), where reality
  diverges (Deviation), what can still be changed (Intervention
  Window), and what inaction costs (Outcome Impact).
- **Experience**: Vertical-specific applications that deliver value in
  context. The same platform, configured differently per industry.

The same underlying capability that enables frictionless checkout in
a grocery store enables crowd management at a stadium, order accuracy
in a QSR kitchen, fraud detection at self-checkout, and biometric
entry at a government border crossing. The behavior is identical. The
context differs.

## Five Product Programs

### Spatial Identity
Frictionless identity verification and access control using spatial
intelligence and optional biometric capture. Eliminates manual
verification bottlenecks while maintaining regulatory compliance and
reducing labor costs at high-throughput entry points.

Variants:
- **Spatial Identity for Government**: CBP Global Entry, border
  control, DHS programs. 2x throughput improvement (120-150 pax/hr),
  >95% TVS compliance accuracy, 99.9% uptime. SI partners: Leidos,
  SAIC. Live with U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP).
- **Spatial Identity for Venues**: Stadium and arena entry via fan
  recognition, integrated with Ticketmaster and AXS. <15 seconds per
  fan entry (5x faster than manual), 40% labor cost reduction per
  event. Live at Intuit Dome (LA Clippers); expanding across NBA,
  MLB, NFL.
- **Spatial Identity for Retail**: Loss prevention, VIP recognition,
  shrink reduction (20-30%). Opt-in model, compliant with BIPA, GDPR,
  CCPA. Pilot planning underway with major retail chains.
- **Spatial Identity for Corporate**: Badgeless employee access,
  visitor management, tailgating prevention, Active Directory
  integration. Targets 50% reduction in unauthorized access incidents.

### Frictionless Commerce
Autonomous checkout: shoppers enter, pick up items, and leave.
Payment is handled automatically — no scanning, no queuing, no
cashier required. The system understands the complete shopping
sequence (entry, product interaction, exit) as a byproduct of
behavioral understanding. The receipt is a byproduct of that
understanding, not the product itself.

Entry modes: app, credit card tap, gated, hybrid. Scales from kiosk
to 10,000 sq ft. POS integrations: NCR, Oracle, Square. Payment
integrations: Stripe, Adyen.

Variants:
- **Grocery & Convenience**: Full-size and micro-format autonomous
  stores. Customers include ALDI, Carrefour, Zabka, Morrisons,
  Choice Market.
- **Venues**: Frictionless concession and merchandise stands inside
  stadiums and arenas. 280+ locations worldwide, 74+ stadium stores,
  7 NFL stadiums, 20+ sports venues. Fastest growing vertical (236%
  growth 2023-2024).
- **QSR**: Autonomous ordering combined with kitchen order assembly
  accuracy monitoring. Detects assembly sequence deviations before
  food leaves the pass.
- **Travel Hubs**: High-throughput retail for time-constrained
  travelers; international payment method support.

### Fraud Prevention
Real-time detection and prevention of fraudulent behavior using
spatial understanding and behavioral analysis. Detects behavioral
patterns — sequence deviations, interaction anomalies, movement
signatures — that correlate with loss events, intervening while
events are still in progress, not after the fact.

Does not infer identity. Operates on behavioral signals only, making
it compliant in jurisdictions with strict biometric privacy laws.

Addresses $100B+ in annual US retail shrink: organized retail crime
(ORC, up 26% since 2020), self-checkout fraud ($10B annual US loss;
23% loss rate vs 7% at traditional checkout), and employee theft.

Variants:
- **Fraud Prevention for Retail**: ORC detection targeting high-value
  goods (electronics, cosmetics, baby formula). Real-time alerts to
  loss prevention teams. Behavioral, not biometric.
- **Fraud Prevention for Self-Checkout**: Skip-scan detection, produce
  fraud identification, coupon fraud. Integrated with POS systems
  (NCR, Oracle, Square).
- **Fraud Prevention for Pharmacy**: Controlled substance monitoring
  (opioids, pseudoephedrine), employee diversion detection,
  compliance-grade audit trail.
- **Fraud Prevention for Returns**: Return fraud and receipt
  manipulation detection. Flags behavioral patterns at the returns
  desk without requiring identity verification.

### Journeys
Historical spatial analytics: customer journey mapping, heatmaps,
dwell time, conversion zones, traffic patterns, staffing optimization,
and space utilization. Designed for operators who need to understand
what happened — to optimize layout, staffing, merchandising, and
experience design. Integrates with Tableau, PowerBI, Snowflake,
BigQuery.

Variants:
- **Retail**: Conversion funnel analysis, dead zone detection,
  merchandising effectiveness, peak vs off-peak staffing alignment.
- **Venues**: Crowd flow maps, concession wait time analysis, fan
  dwell patterns, commercial space performance.
- **Airports**: Passenger flow optimization, security queue analytics,
  gate and lounge utilization, commercial dwell performance.
- **Corporate/CRE**: Desk and meeting room utilization for hybrid
  workplaces, collaboration space effectiveness.

### Co-Pilot
Real-time operational intelligence: live alerts, proactive
recommendations, and automated actions for frontline teams. Shifts
operations from reactive (discovering problems after they occur) to
proactive (intervening while outcomes can still be changed). Powered
by the platform's Reasoning Layer: State, Expectation, Deviation,
Intervention Window, Outcome Impact.

Integrates with Kronos and Workday (workforce management), Slack and
Teams (communications), PagerDuty (alerting).

Variants:
- **Retail**: Unassisted shopper detection, restocking triggers,
  checkout queue alerts, theft anomaly flags.
- **QSR**: Kitchen bottleneck detection, order accuracy monitoring,
  drive-thru wait time alerts, food waste reduction.
- **Venues**: Security threat detection, crowd surge alerts, lost
  child protocols, concession staffing triggers.
- **Airports**: Wheelchair and passenger assistance flagging, security
  queue overflow, gate congestion alerts.

## Technology & Architecture

- **Camera-only**: No shelf sensors or RFID required. RFID can
  enhance but is not a dependency.
- **No facial recognition by default**: Shoppers are tracked using
  anonymous skeletal keypoint detection across 8+ body joints.
  Identity is never inferred in commerce or analytics contexts.
- **Biometric opt-in**: Facial recognition is available selectively
  for Spatial Identity use cases (venue entry, government border
  control, retail VIP) with explicit enrollment and consent.
- **Real-time digital twin**: Context-aware spatial model of the
  entire space, not isolated camera feeds.
- **Edge AI / MEC**: Server-free deployment option using Mobile Edge
  Computing (with Verizon 5G), reducing GPU costs and network
  dependency.
- **Cloud infrastructure**: Built on Microsoft Azure.
- **Privacy by design**: No biometric data stored in commerce
  contexts. GDPR-compliant, CCPA-compliant, ISO 27001 certified,
  TVS-compliant (government).
- **Flexible entry formats**: App, credit card tap, gated, hybrid.
- **Scalable**: Kiosk to 10,000 sq ft; multi-format venue deployments
  (bars, markets, merchandise, main entry gates).

## Competitive Positioning

AiFi is the leading independent spatial intelligence platform. Key
differentiators versus alternatives:

- vs. **Amazon Just Walk Out**: AiFi is retailer-agnostic. Operators
  share no data with a competitor. Camera-only; no shelf sensors.
  Amazon pulled Just Walk Out from its own US Fresh and Whole Foods
  stores in 2024.
- vs. **Trigo**: AiFi requires no shelf sensor infrastructure; Trigo
  does. AiFi has greater depth in venues and government.
- vs. **Grabango**: Shut down in 2024 after failing to secure
  additional funding.
- vs. **Everseen / Appriss Retail (fraud)**: AiFi prevents fraud in
  real time during the event; competitors focus on post-hoc detection
  and analytics.
- vs. **CLEAR / Wicket (identity)**: AiFi combines spatial
  intelligence with identity verification; not a biometric-only
  product. Cross-vertical; no dependency on a proprietary enrollment
  network.
- vs. **RetailNext / Density (analytics)**: AiFi provides
  journey-level granularity, not just zone counts. The same platform
  also supports checkout and fraud prevention — analytics is one
  expression of the platform, not the product.

AiFi is the only platform to achieve this scale (280+ locations)
with a camera-only approach across this range of use cases.
